## [1.5.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

-   Received wishes: two weeks after the owner's birthday, wishes which were fulfilled or claimed up to then leave their lists. They are still shown under Spoilers, with the date they were received, and can be deleted there one by one or all at once. Making a wish available again ("Still need it", or a giver's undo) puts it back on the list
-   "Tidy up after my birthday" in Preferences turns this on or off. It can only be changed when a birthday is set
-   "My gifts" shows when each gift was bought
