## [1.4.0] - 2026-11-07

### Added

-   A "My gifts" page (in the Wishlists menu) listing the wishes you fulfilled for others: what, for whom, which list, the price and when. It keeps them even after the wish or list is deleted
-   Fulfilled a wish by mistake? Undo it for an hour, from the message shown right after fulfilling or from "My gifts". Without logging in, gifts are remembered in the browser for 24 hours and move into your account when you log in
-   A shared wishlist reminds you how many of its wishes you already fulfilled
-   The wish title shows how long it is once it gets near the 128-character limit, and says when it is full, in both the add and the edit dialog

### Changed

-   Wish cards show the picture below the title with a soft vignette, instead of under a white wash. The details and buttons appear on hover, on keyboard focus, or when the card is tapped on a touch screen
-   Looked-up wish titles that are too long are shortened at a word break, leaving room to type

### Removed

-   The unused "Wish temporarily fulfilled" overlay and 30-minute hold. Nothing could start it since the card's Fulfil button was removed; "Fulfil wish" makes a wish unavailable right away, and the person who fulfilled it can undo that for an hour

### Fixed

-   Wish cards not revealing their buttons on hover with an iPad trackpad
-   Wish titles with symbols such as `&` getting their end cut off when saved: the title is counted the way it is stored, and typing or pasting stops at the limit
